# Folio design and acceptance

Primary reference: design/folio-concept.png (generated design concept).

Use a quiet dark writing studio, a thin top bar, left book/chapter rail, central manuscript surface and right writing companion. Dark forest canvas #0c1212, panels #121918, manuscript #18201f, mint accent #86c9aa, muted #9aa9a2, antique gold #d5b875 and fine borders #2b3b36. The original warm concept remains the visual reference, but the shipped default is dark at the author's request. EB Garamond literary text and DM Sans readable UI. Buttons 13px, document 21px with 1.7 line height. Restrained 4-8px rounding, thin lucide icons, minimal shadows. Green active navigation with a darker selected surface. No marketing page.

Reference copy: Folio; Your story, thoughtfully told.; Saved to this computer; Export; The House of Tides; A novel by you; Manuscript; Story bible; Illustrations; Chapters; The letter; A familiar stranger; What the sea kept; My library; Focus mode; A little inspiration; Big stories begin with small ideas.; Write; Refine; Your idea; Continue the story; Build a scene; Length; Pages; Voice & atmosphere; Lyrical & intimate; Develop this idea; Make it your own.

Functional necessities extending the concept: real computed counts, full book settings, language/RTL, recoverable saves and errors, export/import, chapter status/reorder/version controls, prompt length constraints, voice sample, generation review/cancel, automatic loopback Qwen3.8 27B selection when a local model server is present, a high-precision Qwen3.5 on-device model attempt with bundled fallbacks, lazy Janus browser art generation with deterministic offline fallbacks, illustration gallery and optional provider connection settings. Chapter prose is an original editable sample and labeled as such in library. Font sizing and controls adapt to available viewport. Library, story bible, gallery and dialogs use same design system.

Acceptance: create/edit/switch books and chapters, formatting/undo/redo, saved reopening, recovery/conflict protection, named revisions/restore, no-setup words/lines/pages writing and summaries with story/voice context, selection refinement and reviewed insertion, clear offline/provider error and cancel behavior, grounded browser/local/hosted image generation prompts/gallery/insertion, standalone backup with image portability, text/Markdown/HTML/DOCX/EPUB and print/PDF route, responsive and keyboard accessible views. Test backend persistence/security, prompt/result handling, exports and key UI workflows.
