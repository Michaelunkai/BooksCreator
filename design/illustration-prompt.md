# Sample illustration brief

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the editable example book; saved as `public/assets/tide-illustration.png`.

An old stone house far away on a rugged Cornish headland, seen from below across curling sea waves and dark craggy rocks, a few tiny gulls, no people. Traditional copperplate engraving and pen-and-ink book illustration with subtle watercolor washes in muted forest green, slate and warm sand. Antique ivory paper; plentiful blank sky, scene occupies lower two thirds, delicately feathered edges. Quiet, mysterious, wistful, fine linework and crosshatching. A literary chapter opener about a letter from a house that no longer exists. No text, lettering, border, watermark or digital clipart.

The concept source is `folio-concept.png`. All controls in the delivered app are real HTML/React components.
